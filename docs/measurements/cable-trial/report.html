Real-world trials: over the air and over a cable

Branch AI_deep_refactoing, trials of 5 and 6 November 2026. Tickets #21 and #28.

In short. Over a real audio cable, all three Presets reach 95 % of Messages received at exactly the simulator's threshold (fast -13 dB, standard -16 dB, max-range -22 dB): the sound cards and the cable cost nothing. An apparent loss of 3 to 4 dB in max-range in the first runs was a flaw of the trial itself: the application deleted its oldest kept receptions beyond its 200 MB limit, and the lowest levels are sent first. No Message was ever accepted wrongly.

What was tested

  1. Simulator thresholds (ticket #13): 100 trials per level on white noise, 12-byte Messages hidden up to 10 s into the recording, frequency shift of ±1,100 Hz. The grey lines below.
  2. Over the air, in a room (two computers, loudspeakers and the microphone of a laptop, a little background noise): the three Presets worked well. Short Messages were all received; a 10-Fragment Message in fast arrived with only 5 Fragments at -5 to -7 dB, about 7 dB worse than the simulator, probably reverberation (not demonstrated). The same recordings showed false alarms from keyboard and loudspeaker clicks, which a detector fix removed (per-symbol cap and contrast in the Preamble search; test tests/test_clicks.py), with the thresholds unchanged.
  3. Over a cable (this report): the sending laptop adds white noise digitally at an exact SNR (bench.cable_trial), plays it through its sound card (48 kHz) and a cable into an audio interface on the receiving tower, running the application with "Keep" on. Each Message names its own SNR, so the report needs no list. 20 Messages per level (10 for max-range), levels from 2 dB under the simulator's threshold to 8 dB over it.
  4. Checks on the max-range gap: a finer 1 dB sweep, the clock drift between the two cards, the exact sent audio decoded again digitally (batch decoder and live receiver path), and a continuous recording of the cable decoded offline. They led to the cause: the storage limit of the receiving application (see the Max-range section). Run 5 repeats the sweep with the limit raised to 3,000 MB.

Max-range

The first runs seemed to lose 3 to 4 dB (threshold -18 dB in run 1, -19 dB in run 2), and in run 4 the live receiver read 5 of 10 Messages at -22 dB that an offline decoding of the same continuous recording read 10 of 10. The cause is the trial, not the chain: the receiving application keeps the audio of what it receives up to a limit (200 MB by default) and deletes the oldest receptions beyond it. The Messages are sent from the lowest SNR up, so the lowest levels were the ones deleted: 25 receptions in run 1, 36 in run 2, 15 in run 4 (the numbering of the kept files starts at that number). They were counted as lost. The levels below show only what survived; the Messages that survived at -20 dB were read (4 of 5 in run 1, 4 of 4 in run 2), and replaying the whole run 4 recording through the live receiver gives the same 4, 10, 10, 10 and 9 of 10 (-23 to -19 dB) as offline decoding. Run 5 sends 6 levels of 10 Messages with the limit raised to 3,000 MB and nothing is deleted: the threshold is -22 dB, the simulator's.

Also checked, with the same audio that had been sent: decoding it again digitally (10 of 10 from -22 dB, 4 of 10 at -23 dB), the application's live path on that audio (the same), the two cards' clocks (17 ppm, about 1 ms over a Transmission) and the receiving card's noise (-103 dBFS). None of them was a cause. The recording of run 4 at -19 dB offline is left out: it ended before its last Messages were played.

Conclusions

  • Fast and standard match the simulator to the 2 dB step of the trial: 20 of 20 at the simulator's threshold, 0 of 20 two dB below. The curve is a cliff, not a slope.
  • Nothing above the threshold was lost in any Preset: no sound-card gaps, no send errors, and the SNR estimated by the receiver for the Frames read is within 0.2 dB of the one imposed.
  • The cable is not the room: the 7 dB lost over the air in fast is acoustic (loudspeaker, microphone, reverberation), not the modem and not the sound cards.
  • Max-range matches the simulator too: with nothing deleted (run 5), 10 of 10 from -22 dB up, 6 of 10 at -23 dB, none at -24 dB: a threshold of -22 dB, 0.0 dB lost. The 3 to 4 dB loss of runs 1 and 2 and the live receiver reading fewer Messages in run 4 were one flaw: the lowest levels, sent first, were the receptions the storage limit deleted (bench.cable_trial report now warns).
