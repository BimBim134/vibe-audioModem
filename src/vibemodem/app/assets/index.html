Settings

Station

Written in front of each message you send, like F4JBO: hello. Add /P when you operate portable (F4JBO/P): the history keeps it, so you can tell later which messages were sent from a portable station.

Audio device

Modulation

History

The Messages sent and received are kept on this computer and shown again when the application starts. Only text and figures are kept, never sound. The Export CSV link above the conversation downloads them for a spreadsheet.

Listening band

Only the Signals entirely inside this band of audio are read. Use it to leave out the low-frequency noise of a radio, or to pick the part of the audio where the conversation takes place. It applies to the next pass, also while listening. Leave both empty to listen to everything.

Lead-in

A plain tone sent before each Transmission, at the frequency of its lowest tone, so that a squelch or a VOX is open when the Signal starts. It counts in the airtime. Off at 0.

Push to talk

Key the radio through Hamlib: start rigctld for your radio, then switch this on. The radio is keyed before the Lead-in and released after the end of the sound. Leave it off to use a VOX.

TX blanking

While the station sends, it does not listen, so that its own Transmission, heard back through the audio interface, is not shown as received. The input is heard again this long after the end of the sound, besides the delay of the sound card. A station that answers at once needs it short; a radio with a long squelch tail needs it longer.

Stored receptions

For debug purpose only, each message reception can be stored with 3 s around each Signal. The oldest are deleted first when the limit is reached.
